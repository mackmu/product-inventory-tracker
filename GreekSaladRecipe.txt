🥗 Refreshing Greek Salad
Category: Side / Vegetarian

Prep Time: 15 mins | Cook Time: 0 mins

Ingredients:

2 Large tomatoes (chopped)

1 Cucumber (sliced)

1/2 Red onion (thinly sliced)

1/2 cup Feta cheese (cubed)

1/4 cup Kalamata olives

Instructions:

Combine all vegetables and olives in a large bowl.

Drizzle with olive oil, dried oregano, and red wine vinegar.

Gently toss in feta cheese so it doesn’t crumble too much.
