🍌 3-Ingredient Banana Pancakes
Category: Breakfast / Healthy

Prep Time: 5 mins | Cook Time: 10 mins

Ingredients:

2 Ripe bananas

2 Large eggs

1/2 tsp Cinnamon

Instructions:

Mash bananas in a bowl until smooth.

Whisk in the eggs and cinnamon until well combined.

Cook small spoonfuls on a non-stick griddle over medium heat until golden on both sides.
