Sheet Pan Salmon and Asparagus

This recipe serves 6 as a main course and can be prepared in about 25 minutes (10-12 minutes bake time, plus prep).

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs salmon filet, cut into six 6-ounce portions
  • 2 lbs asparagus, fibrous ends removed
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small lemon, sliced into rings for garnish
  • Most of a small amount of butter (exact amount not specified in snippet, but implied for spreading)

Instructions

  1. Prep: Preheat your oven to 450°F and set the oven rack in the top third to give the salmon some color. Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper, trimming it to fit so it doesn't extend beyond the pan edges (this helps prevent charring under the broiler).
  2. Arrange Ingredients: Place the salmon fillets in a row down the center of your lined baking pan. Arrange the trimmed asparagus on the sides of the salmon.
  3. Season Asparagus: Drizzle the asparagus lightly with olive oil and roll to coat it evenly. Sprinkle both the asparagus and salmon with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Add Butter and Lemon: Spread most of the butter over the salmon fillets and dab the remaining butter on the asparagus. Top each salmon fillet with a lemon slice.
  5. Bake: Bake for 10–12 minutes (this time may vary depending on the thickness of your salmon). You can finish the dish under the broiler for 2–3 minutes for a golden top. Bake until the salmon is flaky and cooked through. According to USDA guidelines, salmon should reach an internal temperature of 145°F as measured with an instant-read thermometer.
  6. Serve: Once cooked, remove from the oven and let rest for 5 minutes before dividing into plates.
