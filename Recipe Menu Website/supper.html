Garlic Herb Chicken with Roasted Vegetables

Ingredients:

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 cup sliced carrots
  • 1 cup diced potatoes

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a small bowl, mix olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, salt, and pepper.
  3. Place the chicken breasts on a baking sheet and brush with the garlic herb mixture.
  4. Arrange broccoli, carrots, and potatoes around the chicken.
  5. Drizzle remaining olive oil mixture over the vegetables.
  6. Bake for 25–30 minutes or until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).
  7. Remove from oven and let rest for 5 minutes before serving.
