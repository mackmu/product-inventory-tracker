Classic Blueberry Pancakes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1/2 cup fresh blueberries

Instructions:

  1. In a bowl, combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until combined.
  4. Gently fold in the blueberries.
  5. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  6. Pour 1/4 cup of batter onto the pan for each pancake.
  7. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook until golden brown.
  8. Serve warm.
