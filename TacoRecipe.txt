🌮 15-Minute Street Tacos
Category: Lunch / Mexican

Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 5 mins

Ingredients:

1 lb Ground beef or steak strips

8 Small corn tortillas

1/2 White onion (diced)

Fresh cilantro

1 Lime (cut into wedges)

Instructions:

Brown the meat in a skillet with salt and cumin.

Warm tortillas on a dry pan until charred slightly.

Fill tortillas with meat, top with onion and cilantro, and squeeze lime over the top.
